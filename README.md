# Fitness and Health Tracking Analysis | Power BI

##  Project Overview

The Fitness and Health Tracking Analysis project explores physical activity, workout duration, recorded calories burned, sleep patterns, dietary preferences, and demographic characteristics using Power BI.

The project aims to transform fitness and lifestyle data into meaningful insights through interactive visualisations, key performance indicators (KPIs), and comparisons across demographic groups.

##  Key Findings

The analysis explored physical activity, workout duration, recorded calorie expenditure, sleep patterns, activity categories, dietary preferences and demographic characteristics across 350 records.

### 1. Daily Physical Activity
- The average daily step count was approximately 10,944.
- The 36–50 age group recorded the highest average daily step count among the age groups analysed.
- Daily step counts varied across age groups and recorded activity categories.

### 2. Workout Duration and Calories Burned
- Average recorded workout duration was approximately 61.4 minutes.
- Average recorded calorie expenditure was approximately 958 calories.
- Workout duration and recorded calorie expenditure showed a strong positive correlation within the dataset.
- Daily step counts also showed a positive relationship with recorded calorie expenditure.

### 3. Activity Category Analysis
- Yoga and walking were the most frequently recorded activity categories.
- Gym activity had the highest average recorded calorie expenditure among the five activity categories.
- Yoga recorded the highest average daily step count among the activity categories.

### 4. Sleep Patterns
- Average recorded sleep duration was approximately 6.6 hours.
- Sleep duration showed very little linear correlation with daily steps, workout duration or recorded calorie expenditure in this dataset.

### 5. Demographic Analysis
- The 18–25 age group was the largest age category represented.
- The 36–50 age group recorded the highest average workout duration and average calorie expenditure.
- The dataset included Male, Female and Other gender categories, allowing comparisons across the recorded groups.

### 6. Dietary Preferences
- Vegetarian was the most frequently recorded dietary preference, followed closely by Keto.
- The Vegetarian category recorded the highest average daily step count and average calorie expenditure among the dietary preference groups.

### 7. Analytical Interpretation
The analysis demonstrates how Power BI can be used to compare activity patterns, explore relationships between numerical variables and identify differences across demographic and lifestyle categories.

These findings describe patterns within the supplied dataset. They do not establish that a particular activity or dietary preference causes higher physical activity or calorie expenditure.

## Data Quality and Limitations

- The dataset contains 350 records and nine columns.
- No missing values or duplicate rows were identified during the initial data-quality review.
- The dataset's source and provenance have not been independently verified.
- The unit used for workout duration was not specified in the supplied data; therefore, workout duration is reported in units rather than minutes.
- Recorded calorie expenditure should not be interpreted as a clinically verified measurement.
- The dataset does not contain dates, so changes over time cannot be assessed.
- Findings should not be generalised to the wider population or interpreted as evidence of causal relationships.

## Data Quality and Limitations
